## 2. Decisions

1. OSM is the route source; OpenSkiData runs were used only as a cross-check (OpenSkiData CO: 38 runs with use `skitour`; OSM: 51 ways with piste:type skitour or skitour;nordic). Reason: same OSM origin (duplicating would double-count) and OpenSkiData feature ids are content hashes that change daily. OpenSkiData is the ski-area source because it merges OSM `landuse=winter_sports` with Skimap.org records and statuses (operating, abandoned, proposed).
2. Lift-served context is spatial, not tag-based: OSM `piste:grooming=backcountry` is used for in-bounds expert terrain throughout Colorado resorts (Crested Butte Extreme Limits, Breckenridge Lake Chutes, Silverton Mountain), and many of those ways are outside resort polygons. Rule: 500 m from operating downhill ski-area polygons, 3 km from operating or proposed ski-area points that have no polygon (this catches private clubs and cat-ski hills such as Cimarron Mountain Club, Ski Stout, Three Forks Ranch, Black Mountain, Irwin), 1 km from any lift. Abandoned ski areas are not lift-served.
3. Undirected OSM ski-tour ways default to `ascent_skin`; the source never says up or down. `ski_descent` is used only for downhill pistes outside lift-served context and for tour ways whose own name names a descent feature. `classBasis` records the rule per feature.
4. Groomed nordic (classic/skating) and nordic with unknown grooming are treated as pistes and excluded; only explicitly ungroomed nordic (`backcountry`, `no`, `user_compacted`) becomes `approach`.
5. OSM `ski=yes` etc. on a path is accepted as a winter-use source tag (approach); a path with no such tag is never used.
6. YAML pitfall found and fixed: bare `yes` in YAML parses as boolean true, which silently dropped every `ski=yes` way. The access list is quoted and a test guards it.
7. GNIS: OSM peaks/passes are matched within 300 m; mismatched names are flagged (`gnisNameMatch: false`) and not resolved. GNIS-only summits and gaps are added with `elevationM: null`.
8. Ski areas without an OSM or Skimap.org id (OpenSkiData-generated nordic clusters) are skipped: no stable id exists.
9. Regions are hand-drawn approximations with priority order; anchor points are tested. Regions added beyond the brief: Sangre de Cristo / Wet Mountains / Spanish Peaks, Grand Mesa; the Flat Tops are grouped with the Northern region (as CAIC groups Steamboat and Flat Tops); the Mosquito Range is in Summit / Tenmile.

10. Land status uses BLM SMA (one generalised multipolygon per agency, about 30 m tolerance, 4.5 MB, public domain). The sources lane recommends PAD-US 4.1 instead, because it carries unit names, designation type (Wilderness, where mechanized travel is banned) and `Pub_Access`. SMA was chosen because its download is a single keyless query and it already answers "who manages this". PAD-US is the follow-up if unit names or wilderness are wanted. Either way the layer is labelled "managing agency only; not current access, legal parking or permission".
11. DEM: the dem* metrics were first computed from AWS Terrain Tiles z13 (256 px, 2017 render). After the lead changed contract section 3.3.1, all dem* metrics were recomputed from Mapterhorn z13 (512 px WebP, about 7.4 m pixels at 39 N, sampled at 7.5 m spacing, 553 tiles fetched 2026-11-06 00:48 to 00:49Z). Aspect is degrees clockwise from true north (the Web Mercator grid). `lengthM` is geometric and unchanged. The route, place and ski-area counts are unchanged.

3. Tests: `pipeline/routes/test_routes.py`, 46 pass (regions, rules, DEM math, schemas, ids, size).
4. DEM pipeline check at 234 OSM peaks with `ele` whose tiles are cached for both DEMs. OSM ele minus bilinear Mapterhorn: median +0.4 m (IQR -2.7 to +3.2). OSM ele minus AWS: median +0.3 m (IQR -0.7 to +1.8). Mapterhorn minus AWS: median 0.0 m (IQR -2.1 to +1.9). OSM peak elevations largely come from NED, the same lineage as AWS, so this verifies georeferencing and decoding, not which DEM is more accurate. Descent aspects (Mapterhorn) match the named faces: Missouri Northface 7 degrees, Harvard South Face 148, Columbia Southwest 188.
5. Discrepancy with `docs/sources/map-routes.md` section 6.1, flagged and not resolved there. That section says named descents such as "Mt. Harvard South Face", "Angel of Shavano (Snow Route)" and "North Maroon Peak Northeast Ridge" are mapped as `piste:type=downhill` + `piste:grooming=backcountry`. In the extract they are `piste:type=skitour` + `piste:grooming=backcountry`. 438 of the 447 downhill + backcountry ways statewide are in-bounds resort terrain.
